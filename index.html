Healthcare That Puts People First

Prime Care Clinic is a neighbourhood practice built around a simple idea: listen carefully, explain clearly, and care for every member of the family.

Whether you come in with a fever, a long-standing condition to review, or a routine check-up, we aim to make each visit straightforward and respectful.

Services for everyday health

Four core services, each with the same approach: a clear conversation with the doctor.

  • General Consultation

    A one-to-one visit with the doctor to talk through any health concern, symptom or question you have.

  • Fever, Cold & Common Illnesses

    Assessment and guidance for everyday illnesses such as fever, cold and cough, for children and adults.

  • BP & Diabetes Check-ups

    Check-ups for blood pressure and diabetes, including a review of your readings with the doctor.

  • Health Check-ups

    Routine check-ups to review your overall health and discuss anything you would like to keep an eye on.
