About the clinic

Prime Care Clinic offers comprehensive care for all ages, from everyday illnesses to routine check-ups.

We are a neighbourhood practice, so we aim to be easy to reach, easy to talk to and clear in our advice. Patients are welcome to bring questions and, where they have them, earlier prescriptions or reports.

Our services include general consultation, care for fever, cold and common illnesses, blood pressure and diabetes check-ups, and health check-ups.

How we aim to care

Three commitments guide how we approach every visit.

Listen first

Every consultation begins with your account of what is happening and what worries you.

Explain clearly

We aim to describe findings and next steps in plain language, so you can ask questions and decide with confidence.

Care for the whole family

From children to older adults, one clinic can look after everyday health across generations.
