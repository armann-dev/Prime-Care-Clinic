Services

Everyday medical care and routine check-ups for patients of all ages.

General Consultation

A one-to-one visit with the doctor to talk through any health concern, symptom or question you have.

Helpful to bring

  • Note down your symptoms and when they began.
  • Bring earlier prescriptions or reports, if you have them.

Fever, Cold & Common Illnesses

Assessment and guidance for everyday illnesses such as fever, cold and cough, for children and adults.

Helpful to bring

  • Mention any medicines already taken.
  • For children, share any temperature readings you have noted.

BP & Diabetes Check-ups

Check-ups for blood pressure and diabetes, including a review of your readings with the doctor.

Helpful to bring

  • Bring recent readings and your medicine list, if you keep one.
  • Mention any recent changes to medicines or routine.

Health Check-ups

Routine check-ups to review your overall health and discuss anything you would like to keep an eye on.

Helpful to bring

  • Bring previous reports for reference.
  • List any concerns you would like to discuss.

These services are for routine care. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services or go to the nearest hospital.
